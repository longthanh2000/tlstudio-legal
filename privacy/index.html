Privacy Policy

All apps by Nguyen Thanh Long · Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy applies to every app published by Nguyen Thanh Long, an individual developer based in Vietnam ("we", "us"), on the Apple App Store (each an "App"). It explains what data the Apps collect, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. Section 2 lists what each App actually uses; a service or permission that is not listed for an App is not used by that App. Questions: tlstudio.apps@gmail.com.

In short: our Apps have no accounts. What you create stays on your device. Some Apps use Google Firebase to understand how they are used, without advertising identifiers. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. Some Apps show ads from Google AdMob; in Apps made for children these ads are non-personalized and child-directed, with no tracking.

1. Data the Apps may collect

  • What you create and your settings (for example drawings) are stored only on your device, inside the App's storage. We do not receive them.
  • Usage data (Firebase Analytics): an app-instance identifier generated by Firebase, app events such as first open, session start and screens viewed, device model, iOS version, app version, language, and approximate country derived from your IP address.
  • App configuration (Firebase Remote Config): the App downloads settings, such as which content is shown or included in a purchase. To do this it sends a Firebase installation identifier, app version, iOS version and language.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): if an App uses it, technical details about a crash: the code location, device model, iOS version and app version.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases and subscriptions are bought and paid for through Apple. Apple processes the payment; we do not receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The App checks your purchase status with Apple on your device and remembers it there.
  • Problem reports and feedback: what you choose to write, an optional screenshot you pick, plus device model, iOS version, app version and language. Section 2 says whether an App keeps reports on your device or sends them to us. Please do not include personal information.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): if an App shows ads (Section 2 says so), the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the device model, iOS version, app version, IP address (used for approximate location), and ad interaction data such as impressions and taps, to deliver ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud. In Apps made for children, every ad request is marked as child-directed: ads are not personalized, are limited to general-audience content, and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA). Buying Premium removes ads.

We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, precise location, the advertising identifier (IDFA), or payment information, unless an App asks you for it for a stated purpose and Section 2 says so.

3. Device permissions

An App asks for a permission only when you use the feature that needs it, and uses it only for that feature:

  • Photos (add only): to save your creations to your photo library when you tap Save or turn on Auto-save. The App cannot read your existing photos.
  • Photo picker: when you attach an image, you choose it in the system picker; the App sees only that image.

You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

6. International transfers

Firebase may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google applies safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses. We keep the transfer records required by Vietnam's Law on Personal Data Protection.

7. How long we keep data

  • Usage data: 2 months in Google Analytics, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • App configuration requests: not stored by us; Firebase keeps installation identifiers as described in its policy.
  • Problem reports sent to us: until the problem is resolved, at most 12 months.
  • Data on your device: until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.

8. Security

Data sent from the Apps is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Only the developer can access usage data, crash reports and reports sent to us. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

Apps marked "made for children" in Section 2 do not knowingly collect personal information from children: no account, no chat, no advertising identifier, no personalized ads, and usage data is not linked to a name or contact. Any ads are requested as child-directed and limited to general-audience content. In these Apps, purchases and the App's own links that leave the App sit behind a parental check. A parent or guardian can email tlstudio.apps@gmail.com to ask about or delete any data.

10. Your rights and choices

  • You can ask to access, correct or delete data about you, object to processing, or withdraw consent, by emailing tlstudio.apps@gmail.com and naming the App. We reply within 30 days.
  • Delete your creations in the App, or uninstall the App to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Turn off features such as Auto-save in the App, or revoke permissions in iOS Settings, at any time.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
  • You can complain to your local data protection authority. In Vietnam this is the Ministry of Public Security.

11. Changes

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date, including when we add an App to Section 2. Important changes will also be shown in the affected App.
