Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Applies to all mobile applications published by TL Studio (Thanh Long Nguyen) ("we", "us", "our").

This Privacy Policy explains what information our mobile applications and games (each an "App") collect, how it is used, who it is shared with and the choices you have. By using an App you agree to the practices described here.

In short: our Apps do not require an account and do not ask for your name, email address or phone number. Game progress is stored on your device. We use third-party services for advertising, analytics and crash reporting, which collect device identifiers and usage data as described below. You can opt out of personalized ads at any time.

1. Information stored on your device

Your creations, game progress, virtual items, settings and preferences are saved locally on your device (and, if you enable it, in your device's own backup such as iCloud or Google backup). We do not upload this data to our servers unless an App explicitly offers a cloud-save or sharing feature and you choose to use it.

2. Information collected automatically

When you use an App, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, country or region, app version, screen size, time zone.
  • Identifiers: advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android, only with your permission where required), app-instance identifiers created by analytics SDKs, and IP address (used for approximate location and fraud prevention, not stored by us).
  • Usage data: screens opened, features used, levels or matches played, session length, in-app events, purchases made (product and date, never card details).
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.

3. How we use information

  • To run the App and remember your progress and settings.
  • To show ads in free versions and to measure them.
  • To understand how the App is used so we can fix bugs, balance gameplay and improve features.
  • To verify purchases and deliver what you bought.
  • To prevent fraud, cheating and abuse, and to comply with legal obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (running the App), your consent (personalized advertising, tracking), and our legitimate interests (analytics, security, product improvement).

4. Third-party services

Our Apps may include the following third-party SDKs. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob / Google Ad Manager (advertising, including mediated networks) — policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses data
  • Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, App Check (Google) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit and Google Play Billing (purchases) — handled entirely by Apple or Google.
  • Some Apps may also use additional analytics or attribution services; these will be listed in the App's store listing "App Privacy" or "Data safety" section.

Ad networks may use your advertising identifier and usage data to show ads that are more relevant to you and to limit how often you see the same ad. You can switch off personalized advertising as described in section 7.

5. Advertising consent

In regions where consent is required (for example the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the App shows a consent form before loading ads, powered by Google's User Messaging Platform. On iOS the App also asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency before using the advertising identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

6. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with: (a) the service providers listed above, to the extent needed to provide their service; (b) authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety and property; and (c) a successor in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under the same protections.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Personalized ads / tracking: iOS: Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising. Android: Settings › Google › Ads › "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization". Where a consent form was shown, you can change your choice from the App's settings screen when available.
  • Analytics: you can reset your analytics identifier by reinstalling the App.
  • Deleting data: deleting the App removes all locally stored data. To request deletion of any data held by our providers that can be linked to you, contact us at the address below.
  • GDPR / UK GDPR: you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information is collected and shared, to delete it, and to opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information. Use the ad settings above to opt out of personalized ads, or contact us.

8. Children

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Where an App is designated for children or mixed audiences in a store, we configure advertising SDKs for child-directed treatment and disable personalized ads for those users. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Data retention

Data on your device stays until you delete the App. Analytics and crash data held by our providers is retained according to their policies (for Firebase Analytics, by default up to 14 months for user-level data). Purchase records are kept as long as needed to deliver your purchases and meet legal requirements.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including transport encryption (HTTPS) for all network traffic. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version takes effect when published in the App or on our website, with the "Last updated" date revised. Material changes will be highlighted in the App where practical.

13. Contact
