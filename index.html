Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your name, then Subscriptions. To restore purchases on a new device, open the app and tap Restore in the Pro screen.

For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
